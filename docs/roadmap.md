# Roadmap

Termite is in active development. These are the next milestones we are working on, in order.

!!! info "In development"
    The features on this page are not available yet. They are planned, and details may change as we build them.

---

## 1. Axis grid and columns in planning

Termite will take the structure of your building into account when it generates a plan. You will be able to give it the **axis grid** and the **columns**, and Termite will place walls and rooms so that they work with the structure: columns stay out of the middle of rooms, and walls follow the grid where it makes sense.

---

## 2. Parking planner

A planner for **parking floors**. From the boundary of the floor, the ramps and the cores, Termite will lay out the parking spaces, the driving aisles and the circulation, and help you fit as many spaces as possible.

---

## 3. Building mass generation from constraints

Termite will generate **building masses** from the constraints of the site, for example the plot boundary, setbacks, maximum height and the area you need to build. You will get mass options to compare before you start planning the floors and units.

---

## 4. Code compliance check agent and final report

An agent that **checks a plan against the rules** that apply to it: your building codes, fire safety distances and internal standards (see [Code compliance](code-compliance.md)). It will point out where a plan does not meet a rule, and produce a **final report** of the check that you can share with your team or attach to your submission.
